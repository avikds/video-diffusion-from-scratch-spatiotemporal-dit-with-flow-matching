Video Diffusion from Scratch: Spatiotemporal DiT with Flow Matching

How it was built

Part 1: Clips with a Known Velocity

A synthetic video dataset you control: a square moving at constant velocity in one of eight directions, a seeded batch generator with motion labels, and a centroid tracker that reads the velocity back from the pixels so that motion can be measured, not eyeballed.

Part 2: Space-Time Tokens

Turning a clip into a sequence the transformer can read: 3-D patchify and its inverse, factorized sinusoidal positional embeddings over space and time, a sinusoidal timestep embedder, and a label embedder with the null class that classifier-free guidance needs.

Part 3: The Spatiotemporal DiT

Multi-head attention, the factorization into spatial attention within a frame and temporal attention across frames with its cost arithmetic, adaLN-Zero modulation, the DiT block, the zero-initialised final layer, and the full video model from clip to predicted velocity.

Part 4: Flow-Matching Training

The straight-path interpolant from noise to data and its constant velocity target, uniform and logit-normal timestep sampling, the flow-matching loss, an exponential moving average of the weights, and the training loop that ties them to the clip generator.

Part 5: Sampling

Classifier-free guidance on the motion label, an Euler ODE sampler from noise to clip, and image-to-video generation by clamping the first frame to its interpolant at every step.

Part 6: Evaluation and the Experiment

Motion metrics from the centroid tracker and a per-frame shape score, a Frechet distance on fixed spatiotemporal features, one training variant as a function, and the experiment that compares the full model with a spatial-only ablation to separate frame quality from motion coherence.
